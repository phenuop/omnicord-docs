What is Omnicord?

Omnicord is a feature-rich Discord bot that brings the world of Ben 10 to your server. Collect aliens, battle other players, join guilds, and build your ultimate team.

100+ Aliens

Collect, trade, and battle with a massive roster of Ben 10 aliens.

Epic Battles

1v1, 3v3, and 4v4 battles with unique abilities.

Guild System

Create guilds, raid others, climb leaderboards.

Tyden Economy

Earn currency, buy items, trade with players.
