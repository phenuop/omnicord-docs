## Step 1: Register

Before you can do anything, you need to register with Omnicord.

### Why Register?

Registration creates your profile and unlocks all features.

### The Command
```
.register
```

<Callout type="info">
You only need to register once. After that, all your progress is saved automatically.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Get Your First Alien

Everyone starts with a Starter Alien from a list of 3 aliens you can choose from.

## Starter Aliens

These are the three aliens available from the Starter Box pool.

| Alien | Species | Type | Rarity | Health | Attack | Defense | Speed | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heatblast | Pyronite | Fire | Common | 80 | 85 | 65 | 75 | 305 |
| Wildmutt | Vulpimancer | Physical | Common | 95 | 90 | 75 | 80 | 340 |
| Stinkfly | Lepidopterran | Air | Common | 75 | 70 | 65 | 85 | 295 |

### Abilities

| Alien | Abilities | Passive Skills |
|---|---|---|
| Heatblast | Flight, Pyrokinesis, Pyro & Cryo Immunity, Fire Tornado Generation, Enhanced speed/strength/durability | Solar Charge (Bronze), Flame Aura (Platinum) |
| Wildmutt | Enhanced Smelling, Durability, Dexterity, Agility, Acrobatics | Feral Instinct (Silver), Heightened Senses (Silver) |
| Stinkfly | Flight, Slime projection, Toxic gas & Saliva, Enhanced strength/durability/agility/speed | Acid Spit (Silver), Toxic Trail (Gold) |

### Type Matchups

| Alien | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Heatblast | Nature, Tech, Physical | Water, Earth |
| Wildmutt | Dark, Tech | Fire, Earth, Air, Psychic |
| Stinkfly | Nature, Physical, Earth | Electric |

### What You Get

<Callout type="info">
Your first alien is free. Additional aliens can be obtained through fragments, boxes, and luckspin.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Start Playing

With your first alien, you can now:

### Battle

Challenge other players with `.battle`.

### Level Up

Fight villains to gain experience and level your alien.

### Earn Rewards

Vote daily with `.vote` and participate in events.

### Join a Guild

Create your own with `.createguild` or join an existing one using `.join` command.
