## Overview

View detailed information about your aliens.

Displays comprehensive information about your specified alien including stats, abilities, experience, and more.

## Usage

```
.info [id]
```

**Aliases:** `.i`

## Information

#### Alien name and ID

#### Current level and experience

#### Base stats (ATK, DEF, SPD)

#### DNA quality

#### Abilities and moves

#### Equipped items

#### Passive abilities

#### Battle performance

## Examples

```
.info - View your active alien info
.info 5 - View info for alien ID 5
```
