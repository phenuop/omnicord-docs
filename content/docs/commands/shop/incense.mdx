## Overview

Manage incense to boost alien spawns in multiple channels (max 5).

The incense system allows you to boost alien spawn rates in specific channels. Perfect for hunting rare aliens!

## Usage

```
.incense [buy | list | pause | resume | stop | pauseall | resumeall | stopall] [channel]
```

**Aliases:** `.incense`

## Subcommands

### buy

Activate incense in the current channel (costs 120 Nullite)

### list

Show all active incense with their channels and remaining time

### pause [channel]

Pause incense in a specific channel

### resume [channel]

Resume paused incense in a specific channel

### stop [channel]

Stop incense in a specific channel

### pauseall

Pause all active incense (Admin only)

### resumeall

Resume all paused incense (Admin only)

### stopall

Stop all incense (Admin only)

## Cost

120 Nullite per incense activation

## Limits

Maximum 5 active incense at once

## Examples

```
.incense buy - Activate incense (costs 120 Nullite)
.incense list - Show active incense
.incense pause #general - Pause incense in #general
.incense resume #general - Resume incense in #general
.incense stop #general - Stop incense in #general
.incense pauseall - Pause all incense (Admin only)
.incense resumeall - Resume all incense (Admin only)
.incense stopall - Stop all incense (Admin only)
```
