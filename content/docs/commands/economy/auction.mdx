## Overview

List, bid on, and manage auctions for aliens and items with a full auction house system.

The auction system lets players list their aliens and items for others to bid on. It's a dynamic marketplace where supply and demand determine value.

## Usage

```
.auction <list|info|start|bid|remove> [args]
```

**Aliases:** `.auc`, `.a`

## Subcommands

### list

View all active auctions with pagination and filters

### list -mine

View only your active auctions

### start

Start an auction with `<alien_id>` `<starting_bid>` `<duration_in_days>`

### remove

Remove your auction if no bids have been placed

### info

View detailed information about a specific auction

### buy

Buyout an auction instantly at the buyout price

### bid

Place a bid on an active auction

## Features

#### Time-limited auctions (up to 10 days)

#### Minimum bid increments system

#### Buyout options for instant purchase

#### Auction history tracking

#### Watchlist for favorite auctions

## Restrictions

#### Auctions can only be used in the support server

#### Maximum auction duration is 10 days

#### Cannot bid on your own auctions

## Examples

```
.auction list - View all active auctions with pagination
.auction start 5 5000 - Start auction for alien ID 5 at 5000 Tyden
.auction bid 12345 5500 - Place bid of 5500 on auction #12345
.auction info 12345 - View detailed info about auction #12345
.auction remove 12345 - Remove your auction (if no bids placed)
.auction buy 12345 - Buyout an auction instantly
```
