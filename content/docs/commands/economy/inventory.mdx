## Overview

View your collected items with pagination.

Displays all items in your inventory with pagination support.

## Usage

```
.inventory [page]
```

**Aliases:** `.inv`, `.bag`, `.items`

## Item Types

#### Potions and boosts

#### Cosmetic items

#### Battle items

#### Eggs

#### Special items

## Features

#### Pagination for large inventories

#### Item rarity display

#### Quantity tracking

#### Item usage options

## Examples

```
.inventory - View your inventory
.inventory 2 - View page 2 of your inventory
```
