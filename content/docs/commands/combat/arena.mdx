## Overview

Live random arena battles against anyone in the queue.

The arena system allows you to battle live against random players who are in the queue. Win battles to climb the ranks and become a Ranker.

## Usage

```
.arena [options]
```

## Features

#### Live random matchmaking

#### Queue system for fair matching

#### Ranker system (Top 10 players)

#### Special titles for Rankers

#### Arena leaderboard

## Rankers

Top 10 players in arena are called Rankers and get special titles based on their position. This is different from battle ranks.

## Examples

```
.arena - Check your arena stats
.arena queue - Join the arena queue
.arena leaderboard - View top arena players
```
