## Overview

Leave your current guild.

Allows you to leave your current guild. You will need to wait 24 hours before joining another guild.

## Usage

```
.leaveguild
```

**Aliases:** `.leave`

## Features

#### Cooldown period of 24 hours

#### Guild stat reset

#### Confirmation requirement

## Examples

```
.leaveguild - Leave your current guild
.leaveguild confirm - Confirm leaving
```
