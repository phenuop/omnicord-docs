## Overview

Raid another guild to steal their Proto-Bits.

Allows your guild's Raiders to raid another guild and steal their Proto-Bits.

## Usage

```
.raid <guild_id>
```

## Features

#### Guild strength comparison

#### Proto-Bit stealing

#### Raid cooldown

#### Defense mechanics

## Requirements

You must have the Raider role in your guild to use this command. Contact your guild owner to become a Raider.

## Examples

```
.raid 12345 - Raid guild ID 12345
.raid - View raid status
```
